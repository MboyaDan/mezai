- Instructions open in a right-side drawer; `?` opens the shortcut dialog.
- Skip asks for a reason (short list plus "other"); Report removes the item from the queue at once.
- No gold result appears after submit; the card collapses into a tick that travels to the progress bar.

Mobile: prompt, then Response A card with its rubric, then Response B card with its rubric (tabs "A | B" pinned under the top bar to jump), preference, rationale, sticky Submit bar. Timer and Skip/Report move into a `⋯` menu.
